Isomer field atlas

Structural isomers: same molecular formula, different connectivity of atoms.

Rotating, reflecting or redrawing a structure does not change its connectivity. Compare bonds, not the direction of lines.

Acyclic alkane: CnH2n+2. Monocyclic cycloalkane: CnH2n. Each carbon has four bonds in total; omitted hydrogens complete its valence.

Find the longest chain or parent ring before naming. These missions practise structural isomerism with the complete acyclic alkane sets through six carbons and selected cycloalkane examples through six carbons. Pearson IAL 4.10–4.11 also requires drawing and naming other cycloalkane isomers through C₆; the ring examples here are not a complete set.
