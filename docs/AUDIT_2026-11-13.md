## Verification performed

- Chemistry checks: graph valence, atom/bond counts, graph equivalence, molecular formulae, longest chain and branch locants.
- 10 origin missions, 20 Naming City missions, 16 Mirror missions, 39 Atlas missions validated in source checks. Formula Ruins retains its original separate teaching/practice sequence.
- Equation answer datasets checked for atom balance; equation entry accepted in actual UI with spaces and ASCII arrow.
- Negative storage tests: malformed JSON and rejected reads/writes; session fallback continued without exception.
- Local HTML link targets checked after moving Formula Ruins.
- Chromium UI: root map, direct late chapter, mission switching, new equation submission and completion update.
- Responsive Chromium iframe checks at requested widths 375/768/1280 (content widths 360/753/1265 due scrollbar) for Atlas screen; no document overflow. At 375 frame, map/Forest/Naming/Mirror and repaired Formula Ruins checked; repaired Ruins measured 360px client/360px scroll width.
- Desktop visual inspection of integrated map; mobile visual inspection of Forest exposed excessive sidebar height, subsequently repaired.

## Remaining limitations

- Later chapters are still dominated by short quiz, sorting, equation and sequencing tasks; decorative art and small exploration panels do not constitute a fully interactive RPG.
- Cycloalkane practice remains selected, not an exhaustive library of all isomers through C6.
- Browser-local progress has no cross-device accounts, teacher analytics or guaranteed persistence after browser data deletion.
- Site audience remains owner-only custom access. Students cannot use it until the owner explicitly changes access.
- No real iOS/WebKit, assistive-technology or classroom testing; no claim of all-device verification.
- User's intended starting screenshot remains unavailable.
