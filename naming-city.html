FORMULA RUINS
FIELD ATLAS

Your naming toolkit

1. Parent: find the longest continuous carbon chain.

2. Number: choose the lowest branch locants; compare at the first difference, not the sum.

3. Branch: one carbon = methyl; two = ethyl.

4. Assemble: numbers + branches + parent. Commas separate numbers; hyphens separate numbers and words.

Use di-, tri- or tetra- for repeated branches. Alphabetise different branches, ignoring these multiplying prefixes.

Ring: use cyclo- when a ring is the parent, e.g. cyclohexane. A single branch needs no locant on a simple ring.

Skeletal reminder: every end and corner is carbon. Carbon-bound hydrogens are omitted.

Pearson IAL 2018 · 4.5, with naming practice supporting 4.9–4.11. Prefixes through C₁₀; alkane/cycloalkane isomer enumeration through C₆ belongs to the next chapter.Official specification ↗
