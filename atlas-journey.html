Refinery: fractionation separates mixtures; cracking breaks large hydrocarbons; reforming rearranges molecules.

Air: CO binds haemoglobin and reduces oxygen transport; SO₂ and NOₓ contribute to acidic rain. CO₂ contributes to climate change.

Fuel: compare the whole life cycle, including extraction, processing, transport and use.

Radical: a species with an unpaired electron, shown by a single dot. Homolytic bond breaking gives one electron to each atom.

Mechanism: initiation generates radicals; propagation consumes and regenerates a radical; termination combines radicals. Use single-headed curly arrows for one-electron movement.
