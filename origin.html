Hazard: the potential to cause harm. Risk: the likelihood of harm and its severity in the particular circumstances.

A risk assessment considers the material, amount, exposure and controls. Reduce risk by using less, suitable containment or ventilation, removing ignition sources, protective equipment and, where suitable, a less hazardous alternative. Controls reduce risk; they do not change the substance’s inherent hazard.

A homologous series has the same functional group and general formula; successive members differ by CH₂, have similar chemical properties and show a trend in physical properties.

Alkanes and cycloalkanes are saturated hydrocarbons. Acyclic alkanes have CnH2n+2; monocyclic cycloalkanes have CnH2n.
